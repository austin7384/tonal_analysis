INPUTS
------
`author' (tempfile) — author-level panel dataset with LLM readability scores (_llm_readability_score), t (submission count / career position), and Female indicator

OPERATIONS
----------
- Loads the author-level tempfile.

- Produces a `binscatter` plot of _llm_readability_score against t (the t-th article in career), separated by Female:
    - Two series: Male (pfblue) and Female (pfpink)
    - Legend labels: "Male" (series 1), "Female" (series 2)
    - X-axis titled "{it:t}th article" (south-east placement, medium-small size); no separate xtitle argument overrides this
    - Y-axis titled "LLM Readability"
    - Aspect ratio 0.6; uses publishing-female scheme and Avenir-Light font face

- Exports the figure as a PDF.

OUTPUTS
-------
~/tonal_analysis/outputs/figures/Figure-4-llm.pdf — Binscatter plot of mean LLM readability score by career publication number t, separately for male and female authors
